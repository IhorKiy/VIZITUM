# Pilot Onboarding Checklist (Customer Admin)

One-page checklist for a customer's Company Admin to complete before the pilot measurement window starts. Content is fixed by `docs/specs/pilot-readiness-spec.md` — do not add or remove steps without a product-owner decision.

Send this to the customer admin directly, or walk through it together on the kickoff call. Each step links to the in-app screen that does it.

## Setup checklist

- [ ] **Confirm company settings.** Check the company display name and time zone under `Settings`, and confirm whether your team tracks individual products/SKUs (toggle in the same screen). If unsure, leave products enabled — it can be turned off later.
- [ ] **Invite your team.** Invite or activate at least one **Team Manager** and at least one **Field Representative** under `Users`. You can invite more later; the pilot only needs one of each to start.
- [ ] **Add your locations.** Import your location list under `Imports`, or add a handful manually under `Locations`. At least one location must be **active** before a field visit can be planned.
- [ ] **Add your products, or mark them not applicable.** If you track products/SKUs, import your catalog under `Imports`. If you don't, mark products not applicable in `Products` (or leave the toggle off in Settings).
- [ ] **Create an initial visit or task plan.** Import a route/task plan under `Imports`, or have a Field Representative start a visit manually from their workspace. This gets real activity flowing before the measurement window starts.
- [ ] **Complete one field visit with a confirmed report.** Have a Field Representative complete a visit end-to-end: start the visit, add a note (text or voice), and confirm the report — either from the AI draft or the manual fallback fields. This confirms the full loop works for your team before the pilot week counts toward the metrics.
- [ ] **Review the manager dashboard and pilot review output.** Have a Team Manager open `Manager` (dashboard) and a Company Admin open `Review` (pilot review) at least once. This is also the first data point counted toward the "manager review usage" pilot metric.

## What happens next

Once this checklist is complete, the 7-day pilot measurement window starts from your team's first confirmed field visit. See `docs/specs/pilot-readiness-spec.md` for the exact success thresholds we'll review together at the end of the week, and `docs/runbooks/pilot-support-process.md` for how to reach us if something blocks your team during the pilot.
